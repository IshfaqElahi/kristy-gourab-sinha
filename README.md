# Kristy Gourab Sinha — Academic & Research Portfolio

Minimal, modern academic and research portfolio website for **Kristy Gourab Sinha** (Department of Materials and Metallurgical Engineering, BUET & Department of Nuclear Science and Engineering, MIST).

## Features
- **Typography**: Crafted with [Manrope](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope) (weights 300–800) and tabular numerals.
- **Design**: Swiss editorial two-column layout with sticky section indices, subtle hairline borders, and adaptive Light/Dark themes.
- **Interactive Filtering**: Filter publications across Journal Articles, Book Chapters, Conference Proceedings, and Presentations, with one-click citation copying.
- **Minimal Motion**: Lightweight `IntersectionObserver` scroll-reveal and subtle micro-transitions respecting `prefers-reduced-motion`.
